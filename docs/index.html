Quick Launcher A Raycast-style launcher for GNOME: passwords, Clockify, calculator

One shortcut, Ctrl Shift Enter, and one search over your Bitwarden and 1Password vaults, your recent Clockify entries and a set of commands. Anything that looks like a calculation, a unit, a date or a time zone is answered as you type.

It follows the shell theme in light and dark and with your accent color, and blurs the desktop behind it. No apps are launched and there is no clipboard history. It is meant for the things a general app launcher doesn't do well.

What it does

  • Passwords from both vaults: Bitwarden through rbw, 1Password through the op CLI. Each row carries the vault's logo. Enter copies the password, Ctrl C the username, Shift Enter types it, even on Wayland.
  • Unlock in place: a locked vault is a row. Type the master password into the launcher, or let the 1Password app ask.
  • Calculator: math and percentages, every unit into every other (2 min in year, 30 mpg in l/100km), currencies at the ECB's daily rates, dates and countdowns (days until christmas, kw), Unix timestamps and time zones (5pm berlin in tokyo). English and German both work.
  • T3 Code: active sessions show up in the search with whether the agent is working, and for how long; Enter opens the session in T3 Code.
  • Clockify: restart a recent entry or start a new one with @project and #tag; the running timer stops at the same second.
  • Emoji and system commands: :rocket copies 🚀, and lock, suspend, power off, dark mode, Do Not Disturb and Night Light are a few keystrokes away.
  • Every action at hand: Ctrl K lists all actions of the selected item with their shortcuts.

Install

Needs GNOME Shell 48, 49 or 50, and rbw and/or op set up. Download the zip from the latest release, then go to your Downloads folder:

cd ~/Downloads

Run:

gnome-extensions install --force quick-launcher@julian.falcondev.de.shell-extension.zip

Log out and back in (Wayland cannot reload the shell in place), and enable it:

gnome-extensions enable quick-launcher@julian.falcondev.de

The shortcut, vaults, the 1Password unlock method, the default currency and the Clockify API key are in gnome-extensions prefs quick-launcher@julian.falcondev.de.
